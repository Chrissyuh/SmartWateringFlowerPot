Hardening observations
- Native USB data and backup J7 boot/debug path are present in PCB net data.
- Pump is switched low-side; ESP32 GPIO only drives the MOSFET gate through a resistor and pulldown.
- USB-C CC resistors are sink-identification resistors only; they do not limit pump or ESP current.
- No dedicated USB input fuse/current limiter or USB data ESD array was found in placed Board A parts.
- No added board-level ESP32 local bulk capacitor near U1 was found beyond the regulator output capacitors and module internal capacitance.

Result
CAD checks remain clean. Ordering decision should depend on accepting Rev A without fuse/ESD/local U1 bulk ECO, plus strict bring-up current limiting and pump-disconnected first flash.
